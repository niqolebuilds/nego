Negotiation Intelligence

For Siloam procurement

Walk into every negotiation knowing the best price you can get.

The engine checks what every Siloam hospital has paid, what competing vendors have offered, and the best price on record, then gives you the price to ask for.

How it works

  1. 1

    Say what you need

    Ask about one product and one vendor, or open the renewal calendar to see which contracts end in the next 30–90 days.

  2. 2

    Get three price options

    Stretch to open with, Target to win, Fallback to hold. Each one beats what Siloam and competitors have paid, and shows the proof.

  3. 3

    Negotiate or look deeper

    Negotiate gives you a plan: your opening line, what to trade if they push back, and your leverage. See details shows the evidence.

Good to know

  • Prices are per clinical unit (one test, one piece, one vial) and also shown in the vendor's pack, so a box of 50 is never compared with a single.
  • The walk-away price is only a proposal. It needs sign-off from someone other than the negotiator before the engine will say accept, push or walk.
  • Amounts are in rupiah: Rp 9.905 per unit, Rp 5,36 M a year (jt = juta, M = miliar).
